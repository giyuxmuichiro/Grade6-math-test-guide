Prime & Composite Guide

Prime vs. Composite Numbers

Learn how numbers are built from factors and test any whole number instantly.

🌟 Prime Numbers

Exact 2 Factors

A whole number greater than 1 that has only two factors: 1 and itself (e.g., 2, 3, 5, 7, 11, 13, 17, 19).

🧱 Composite Numbers

3+ Factors

A whole number greater than 1 that has more than two factors (e.g., 4, 6, 8, 9, 10, 12, 15).

💡
Special Case Warning: 0 and 1 are neither prime nor composite. 1 only has 1 factor (itself), and 0 has infinitely many factors. 2 is the only even prime number!

Algebra Guide

Solving for X & Basic Equations

Algebra is simply solving puzzles where one piece is hidden (called $x$). Your goal is always to keep both sides of the equation balanced.

💡 Golden Rule of Algebra

Whatever you do to one side of the equals sign must be done to the other side!

Worked Example Step-by-step whiteboard
Solve for $x$:    3x + 5 = 17

Step 1: Subtract 5 from both sides: 3x = 12

Step 2: Divide both sides by 3: x = 4

📹Video Lesson: Algebra Basics

Math Antics / Algebra ~11 mins

Key Video Takeaways

  • Inverse operations undo each other (addition undos subtraction).
  • Always perform operations in reverse order of operations when solving for $x$.

✨ Practice Question

Solve for $x$:   2x - 4 = 10

Fractions Guide

Adding & Subtracting Fractions

Fractions represent equal parts of a whole shape or number.

🍕 Common Denominators

Match the bottom numbers before adding or subtracting top numbers!

Worked Example Step-by-step
Add:    1/4 + 2/3

Common denominator = 12: 3/12 + 8/12 = 11/12
